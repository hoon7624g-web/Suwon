National Agricultural Museum FAQ

Is National Agricultural Museum closed on Mondays?

Yes, it is closed every Monday, on January 1, and on Lunar New Year and Chuseok days.

Until what time can I enter National Agricultural Museum?

Last admission is at 17:00.

How much is parking at National Agricultural Museum?

Parking is available and free.
