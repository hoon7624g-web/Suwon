Seo Suwon Sutbul Galbi

In Korean: 서수원숯불갈비

The signature dish at Seo Suwon Sutbul Galbi is premium hanwoo marbled sirloin; the menu also includes homemade pork galbi, chuck flap, skirt steak platter, and more.
