Samsung Innovation Museum FAQ

Is Samsung Innovation Museum closed on Sundays?

Yes, it is closed every Sunday, on legal public holidays, and on substitute holidays.

Do the opening hours of Samsung Innovation Museum differ by day?

Yes, it is open 13:00–14:00 on weekdays and 10:00–17:00 on Saturday.

What activities can I do at Samsung Innovation Museum?

You can try the online children's lab, the family smart classroom, the electronics industry class, and more.
