Gyeonggidaehakgyososeong Museum FAQ

Is Gyeonggidaehakgyososeong Museum closed on Saturdays?

Yes, it is closed every Saturday–Sunday, on legal public holidays, during the Seollal and Chuseok holidays, and on the university founding anniversary (November 8).

What are the opening hours of Gyeonggidaehakgyososeong Museum?

Hours are 10:00–17:00 during the semester and 10:00–15:00 during vacations, among others.

What is the inquiry phone number for Gyeonggidaehakgyososeong Museum?

For inquiries, call 031-249-8901.
