01

52 arboretum, lake, museum, and Aqua Planet sights and 43 cultural facilities

  • In Korea Tourism Organization TourAPI data, 52 Suwon attractions have arboretum, lake, park, or museum in their names, such as Suwon Arboretum Irwol, Yeongheung Arboretum, Gwanggyo Lake Park, Aqua Planet Gwanggyo, and National Agricultural Museum.
  • The data also lists 43 places classified as cultural facilities.

02

Check regular Monday closures and whether advance booking is needed

  • Many places, such as Suwon Arboretum Irwol, Yeongheung Arboretum, National Agricultural Museum, Suwon Hwaseong Museum, and Suwon Gwanggyo Museum, are closed every Monday, and Samsung Innovation Museum is visited on weekdays after booking on its website.
