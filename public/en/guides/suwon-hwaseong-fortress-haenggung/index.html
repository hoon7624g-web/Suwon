Check the always-open fortress sections and the ticket sales times of the palace and activity facilities

  • Suwon Hwaseong Fortress Wall Trail and the outside of the four main gates are open year-round free of charge.
  • However, Hwaseong Haenggung Palace, Hwaseong Fortress Tourist Trolley, and the Flying Suwon tethered helium balloon have their own ticket sales cutoffs and weather and closure conditions. It is therefore safer to compare the operating rules of the 10 places below before you set out.
